From signal to action

Cheap rules and models filter first. The language model only writes the message for the customers that remain.

Channel mix

1Signals

Transactions, in-app behaviour, customer data and products, only within the customer's consent.

2Moment detection

Lightweight rules and models per moment produce a confidence score. Runs across millions of customers.

3Decision

≥ 45%: first ask the customer whether it's right, act only after "Yes". < 45%: do nothing. Sensitive moments: never sell. When things get worse (job loss, divorce), KBC only asks an open question: "Has something changed in your life?".

4Channel & timing

In-app message, push notification or an advisor, depending on the moment and the customer's preference.

5Personalisation

A language model writes the message, always with "Why am I seeing this?".

Why this scales

The expensive work (a language model) only happens for the small group of customers where a moment was detected with confidence. Detection itself is cheap and runs in batch or streaming across all customers. Adding a new moment = one new detection rule + one action template, not a new product.
